## Cancelling

Cancel any time, from the billing portal or by emailing us. Cancellation takes
effect at the **end of the period you've already paid for** — you keep what you
bought until then, and you are not charged again.

- **Intelligence:** licence keys are issued for about 40 days and renewed each

  it expires, then the paid features step aside and Serin keeps running as the
  open-source core. Nothing is deleted; nothing stops working except the paid
  additions.
- **Cloud:** your account is suspended at the end of the paid period. **Your
  data is retained, not deleted** — you can export it, and reactivating restores
  access. Ask us to delete the account and we will.
