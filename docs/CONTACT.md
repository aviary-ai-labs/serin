# Contact Serin

*How to reach us, and where to go for the things that have a faster path.*

## Support

**support@serin.money** — questions about your account, billing, a connection
that stopped working, or anything that looks wrong. One person reads this, so
plain description beats formal phrasing: what you did, what happened, and what
you expected instead.

We aim to reply within two business days.

## Security

**security@serin.money** — vulnerability reports, and anything you would
rather not put in a normal support queue. Please do not open a public GitHub
issue for a security problem. See [SECURITY.md](/security) for what we commit
to and what is in scope.

## Bugs and feature requests

Serin is open source. The fastest route for anything reproducible is a GitHub
issue on the [Serin repository](https://github.com/aviary-ai-labs/serin) —
it is public, searchable, and the person who fixes it reads it directly. Use
support@ instead if the report would expose your holdings or account details.

## Things with a faster path than email

| What you want | Where |
|---|---|
| Cancel your subscription | [serin.money/cancel](/cancel) — self-serve, no email needed |
| Export your data | In the app: Connectors → Data → Backup. Works on a lapsed account too |
| Delete your account | In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account |
| Change your password | In the app, from the lock screen's "Forgot password" link |
| Read what we store | [Privacy policy](/privacy) |

## Brokerage connections

Serin connects to brokerages through SnapTrade, read-only. Serin never
receives your brokerage username or password — those go to your broker
through SnapTrade's own portal — and Serin cannot place trades, transfer
money, or change anything at your broker. A connection can be removed at any
time from Brokerages inside the app.

If a connection stops updating, disconnect and reconnect it first; brokers
expire authorizations periodically. If that does not fix it, mail support@
with the broker name and roughly when it stopped.

## Who you are contracting with

Serin Cloud is operated by the maintainer of the Serin project. Serin is not a
broker, a dealer, or an investment adviser, and nothing in the product is a
recommendation to buy or sell — see the [terms](/terms).
